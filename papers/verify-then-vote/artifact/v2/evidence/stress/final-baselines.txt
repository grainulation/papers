  ▸ Verdict
  ························································
  Bran's evidence scoring selects winners based on evidence QUALITY,
  not evidence QUANTITY. Majority vote fails when misconceptions are
  popular (many agents repeat them). Best-confidence fails because
  hallucinators report high confidence. Last-writer-wins is arbitrary.
  Random provides the expected-value lower bound.
