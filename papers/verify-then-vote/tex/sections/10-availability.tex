\section*{Artifact Availability}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Artifact Availability}

Code, Lean proofs, sealed inputs, study protocols, and analysis scripts accompany the paper at \artifactrepo. The artifact includes the matched gate ablation, validation-channel audit, blank human-review packet, and portable source-reconstruction checks. \suppref{S7} and artifact README map results to their reproduction commands. Protocol freezes record when methods and analyses were specified; they are not prior publication versions. Exact public-redaction mappings preserve the relationship to the frozen study records.
