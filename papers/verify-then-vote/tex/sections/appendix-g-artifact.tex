\paragraph{Preregistration and deviations.} The OVERTURN preregistration, its development-stage selections, the reducer binary, the evaluator, the checker identity, and the policy templates were frozen by hash (56 files) before confirmatory collection; all hashes were re-verified before the single confirmatory analysis. A pre-specified non-binding futility look at 300 items read only the corrected and overturned counts of the primary contrast (25 against 4). The local-checker arm (H2) is reported as not run, because the candidate local checker did not reach the validation thresholds before the freeze, and the local judge is exploratory. The client used for every hosted call was pinned to the version in the checker identity, and one check, one reader call, and one closed-book call returned refusals, scored as unchecked and as wrong respectively. Two judge analyses, regeneration and leave-one-agent-out, ran in fresh caches so that each call is new. The evaluator's per-decision check cost counts only calls made in its final step, because earlier calls were served from the shared label cache; the \paperref{main-paper audit and cost section} reports the cost from the complete call logs. The companion memory-only benchmark of \paperref{main-paper passage controls} (600 confirmatory items) was analyzed once with its frozen evaluator after OVERTURN was frozen, as its preregistration required; its amendment-5 analyses (non-inferiority and cost) use a script that imports the frozen test functions, its evidence-verification hypothesis was not run because no confirmatory capture exists, and its timing ran on a loaded machine. Item files and captured passages derived from FEVER keep its CC BY-SA 3.0 license, and other third-party data keep their own licenses, listed with the artifact; full datasets and corpora are represented by their hashes and fetch commands. Model digests for all five agents are recorded in the run manifests.

\paragraph{Second pool.} Its preregistration, pool manifest, and freeze record (82 files) were frozen before confirmatory collection, with verified default first as the primary rule and the OVERTURN hypotheses repeated unchanged; the first pool's labels and its checker, judge, and policy identities are reused byte-identically. One candidate agent failed qualification because its model server stopped on every request, and its first pre-declared alternate replaced it. A two-way local checker considered as a secondary arm was removed before the freeze, because it cannot verify a REFUTED answer under the unchanged claim representation (\cref{sec:prereg}). Four checks returned refusals and are scored as unchecked. The evaluator's per-decision check cost for the second pool (\$0.0083) counts only labels first checked in its confirmatory run, not those from its futility look or reused from the first pool; the \paperref{main-paper audit and cost section} reports the cost from the complete call logs of both pools. The verified-default-first rule, the alias list of the secondary grader, the checker-wording test, and the two-way checker re-decision were specified after the first pool's analysis; each is reported as such. The second pool's model digests and licenses, including the non-commercial license of EXAONE~3.5, are listed in its manifest.
